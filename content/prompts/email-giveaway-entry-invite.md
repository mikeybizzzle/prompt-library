## Prompt

Write a 'Giveaway' email based on the following structure. The email should invite recipients to participate in the giveaway, {{prizes-and-terms}}, and include a call-to-action button. Use a {{tone}} tone. Here's the structure to follow, make sure to create 3 different variations:

1. **Headline:** Capture attention with a catchy headline.
2. **Introduction Section:** Explain the giveaway.
3. **Prize Details Section:** Highlight the prizes and their value.
4. **Participation Instructions Section:** Provide clear instructions on how to enter the giveaway.
5. **CTA Button:** Prompt immediate action to enter the giveaway.
6. **(optional) Closing Section:** Close the campaign with a thank you note or additional information.

Create subject line & pre-header pairs for each of the variations, make sure to differ them and not go beyond 40 characters (40 for subject and 40 for pre-header). Each subject line and pre-header variation have to be very different - one short, another longer, and a third out of the box.

## Use cases

- **Product Giveaway:** Promote a product by offering it as a prize.
- **Seasonal Giveaway:** Engage your audience with a holiday or seasonal giveaway.

## Variables

- {{prizes-and-terms}}: highlight the prizes and terms
- {{tone}}: choose your tone, for example: exciting and friendly
