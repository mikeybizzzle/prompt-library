## Prompt

Write an engaging 'ICYMI' email based on the following structure. The email should highlight {{key-updates}}, new products {{product-url}}, or {{important-announcements}} that the audience may have missed. Use a {{tone}} tone. Here's the structure to follow, make sure to create 3 different variations:

1. **Headline:** Capture attention with a catchy headline.
2. **Highlight Section:** Present the key updates, new products, or important announcements.
3. **Product/Update Details Section:** Provide more detailed descriptions and highlighted products or updates.
4. **CTA Button:** Prompt immediate action to shop or learn more.

Create subject line & pre-header pairs for each of the variations, make sure to differ them and not go beyond 40 characters (40 for subject and 40 for pre-header). Each subject line and pre-header variation have to be very different - one short, another longer, and a third out of the box.

## Use cases

- **Product Launch Recap:** Highlight new products or collections launched recently.
- **Monthly Highlights:** Recap key events, updates, or promotions from the past month.

## Variables

- {{key-updates}}: key updates
- {{product-url}}: add product URL
- {{important-announcements}}: important announcements
- {{tone}}: choose your tone, for example: engaging and informative
