## Prompt

Write an engaging 'Mystery Offer' email based on the following structure. The email should create excitement around a mystery offer or product, highlight the potential rewards (if applicable), and include a call-to-action button. Use a {{tone}} tone. Here's the structure to follow, make sure to create 3 different variations:

1. **Headline:** Create curiosity with a catchy headline.
2. **Mystery Offer Section:** Describe the mystery offer and how it works
3. **CTA Button:** Prompt immediate action to reveal the offer.

Create subject line & pre-header pairs for each of the variations, make sure to differ them and not go beyond 40 characters (40 for subject and 40 for pre-header). Each subject line and pre-header variation have to be very different - one short, another longer, and a third out of the box.

## Use cases

- **Special Promotions:** Offer a mystery discount or special offer that will be revealed upon clicking.
- **Product Promotion:** Tease product in the email, but reveal it on the website, after the click
- **Flash sale:** Tie mystery offers to holiday or special events to enhance excitement and drive traffic.

## Variables

- {{tone}}: choose your tone, for example: playful and intriguing
