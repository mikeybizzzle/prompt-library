## Prompt

Write an engaging 'Featured In' email based on the following structure. The email should highlight recent media coverage or endorsements from reputable sources {{features}}, showcase key quotes or highlights, and include a call-to-action button to explore the featured products. Use a {{tone}} tone. Here's the structure to follow, make sure to create 3 different variations:

1. **Headline:** Announce the media feature with a strong headline.
2. **Introduction Section:** Provide a brief introduction or summary of the feature.
3. **Media Highlights:** Showcase key quotes or highlights from the media coverage.
4. **CTA Button:** Prompt immediate action to explore the featured products.

Create subject line & pre-header pairs for each of the variations, make sure to differ them and not go beyond 40 characters (40 for subject and 40 for pre-header). Each subject line and pre-header variation have to be very different - one short, another longer, and a third out of the box.

## Use cases

- **Media Mentions:** Highlight your brand’s appearance in well-known media outlets.
- **Product Endorsements:** Showcase endorsements from respected publications or influencers.
- **Credibility Building:** Use third-party validation to build trust with your audience.

## Variables

- {{features}}: insert URLs or descriptions of the features here
- {{tone}}: choose your tone, for example: authoritative and proud
